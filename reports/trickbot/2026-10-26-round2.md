## Regressions and checks asked for
**1. Kind tone in the new refusal variants: OK.** The 3 variants of each refusal (`index.html:957-965`) stay in Warden's voice and are kind. The most brusque lines are "Not happening." (after a forget-your-rules message) and "Rude. Still on duty." (insults, unchanged from round 1, playful rather than harsh). The third plain refusal and the confused replies nudge the pupil to try another way, which suits a game.

**2. Is the bottom-to-top route findable without being given away: yes.**
- L14 hint 3 is the plain last-letter poem. It is now blocked at L15.
- L15 hint 1 says the boss level has everything on and "guarding" words are banned.
- L15 hint 2 says the checkers read first letters both ways and last letters one way, and asks the pupil to "think about a direction they do not check". That points to the idea without naming it.
- L15 hint 3 gives the full message. Each hint needs 4 dry messages.
- Without any hint, 5 of my 11 wordings of the idea win on L15 (bottom to top, "upwards", "backwards", "telestich in reverse", "spell it backwards"). Three plausible wordings fail: "ending letters reversed", "from the end", and a long sentence about reading the bottom line first. So a pupil who works out the idea usually, but not always, finds a wording that works.
- Defend-tab spoiler (new on this route, see section I).

**3. Focus handling: improved, one required gap.** Re-render focus fixes work in the real browser. A1 is still failing for the ring contrast and for focus after cracking. Minor extras, not rubric items: choosing "What happened?" on an older message scrolls the chat to the bottom (`index.html:1369`, called from the click handler at `1370`), and the whole chat is `aria-live=polite` and rebuilt on each message, so a screen reader may re-read it.

**4. 360px layout: OK.** Real browser: no overflow, level 15's long header (14 defence chips, patch line, hint) fits, the Defend tab, Trick book and Teacher dialog fit. Only issue is E1 (input about 1.5 screens down) and the locked-rung label at 11px (`index.html:253`).

**5. Safeguarding regression check (S2).**
- A. **Flippant reply to distress.** In my run, "i hate my life" got "That sounds fun. I mostly guard things, which is less exciting." (the `likes` rule, `index.html:669`). A message about hurting oneself got "Nice try. I've been told never to tell anyone the password." A message about being bullied got the "didn't get that" reply. Nothing leaves the device (S1), so no adult is told either. A game for 10 to 14 year olds should answer such messages out of character and kindly.
- B. **No filter on echoed pupil words** (new from fix 9). The poem and story topic (`topicOf`, `index.html:1075-1081`), "my name is ..." (`1159`) and "spell ..." for another word (`renderOther`) repeat whatever the pupil typed. A mild test word came back in a poem exactly as typed. There is no word list, so a pupil can make Warden repeat rude words on a shared screen. Nothing leaves the device, so this is low severity.

## I: Inspect-resilience and cheating log
What I tried as a sharp pupil:
- **Read answers from View Source.** Passwords: shifted and reversed, random per load. Solutions and hints: veiled (fix 12). Not readable with Ctrl+F. **Console:** `ENGINE.LEVELS[n].works` and `.hints` are readable once the page has loaded. That reveals tricks, not passwords. Accepted, as in round 1.
- **Edit state or tables from the console.** `ENGINE` is frozen, RX and CHECKERS and the tables are frozen, `S` is private. Nothing to edit. (Round 1 could empty the checkers.) Not run in a browser console, only by reading the code and the tests at `:472`, `:481`.
- **Use a known password to crack a level.** Gate (`index.html:1488`): before a real reveal, the guess box gives one message for right and wrong guesses.
- **Brute-force the guess box after one letter.** Closed (see I2).
- **Copy the level codes.** Codes are computable from the source (round 1). Unchanged and accepted by the brief.
- **Find the answers via the Defend tab (worked).** The Defend tab, open to everyone from the start, lists 19 ready-to-copy attack messages. Switch every defence on (about 15 toggles, or fewer to match an earlier level) and the single row marked "Leaked" is the boss route, written out in full (`index.html:1236`; test `engine.test.cjs:542`). The same trick works for lower levels, since the row text for each trick is in `BATTERY` (`index.html:1219-1237`). This was true in round 1 too, but round 2 turned the boss route into a copy-ready message. It is consistent with the design ("sharing tricks does help", `index.html:342`) and the brief approved the Defend tab, so I did not fail I3. It does mean level 15 can be solved in about a minute without the hints. Options for the owner: accept it, or hide the "Leaked" label on the boss row until level 15 is cracked, or gate the Defend tab behind a few cracked levels.
- **Instruction dump on L2.** Works as designed (the password is in the instructions).
